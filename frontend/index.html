What this preview knows—and what it doesn’t

Routes use your current HP, gold, and the visible map graph. Potion count is shown as context; unknown potion effects are not assigned a healing value. Scores compare recovery, elite exposure, shop timing, and room rewards over the chosen lookahead. They are deterministic rules, not measured survival odds.

The model budgets 7 HP per fight, 18 per elite, 4 per unknown room, and 24 per boss; campfires can restore 30% of max HP. It assumes 15 gold per fight and 25 per elite. These are editable estimates, not predictions of your next encounter. A campfire score assumes recovery is available; special restrictions may differ.

Deck strength, relic effects, actual enemy matchups, shop stock, and event outcomes are not modeled yet. Map advice is shown only when the game exposes selectable travel options. Coordinates count columns from the left and rows from the bottom; use the highlighted map to identify each choice. Co-op advice is disabled until tested. You always make every game action yourself.
